### The test machine at each size

Native's own numbers on GitHub's four-core machine, which runs the real cluster, the model of the organism and, in Omni-Compass's arm, Omni-Compass's math for every modelled muscle, all at once:

| Size | Machine busy (native) | Failed requests (native) | The cluster's CPU, Omni-Compass's arm against native | Off the window's clock |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 copy (100 repetitions) | 69 to 75% | 6 to 10% | -1.7 to -0.7% | none |
| 10 copies (100 repetitions) | 82 to 85% | 22 to 31% | -2.5 to -1.2% | none |
| 100 copies (100 repetitions) | 87 to 91% | 32 to 43% | -7.0 to -4.0% | The whole tower 1 of 100, The four stacked 49 of 100 |

The load is the same at every size; what grows with the copies is the model on the same four cores. Where the machine runs out, native's own failed requests rise with it, the cluster gets less CPU in the arm whose model also carries Omni-Compass's math, and the largest organisms fall behind the window's clock, so their last steps no longer follow the scheduled load: those squares read the test machine's limit, not Omni-Compass governing a cluster. They run again on the rewired test, the model on cores of its own.
