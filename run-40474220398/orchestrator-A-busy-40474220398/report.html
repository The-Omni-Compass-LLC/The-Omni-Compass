Omni-Compass's own orchestrator: the first showing

Built from scratch on 23 October 2026: Python's standard library and Omni-Compass's own compass law, no code of Kubernetes or any other orchestrator. Run on this one machine, every piece a real process, every answer checked. Local only, not pushed. Run and scored by The Omni-Compass LLC's own code. No outside party has reviewed or approved these results.

The rig. Six machines of half a processor each, walled by the kernel's own CPU quotas (two machines on each of three cores); the control plane, the front, the six machines' hands and the clients on the fourth core. The service's units: 250 millicores each (request and limit on the yardstick, as Kubernetes holds them; on Omni the wall is conveyed to the room the machine can spare). Each request: a chain of 80,942 SHA-256 hashes (about 40 ms of processor), its digest checked by the client. The clients: closed loop, 0.1 s of thought between requests, stepping by one.

1. Native, Omni on native and Omni direct, the same clients

Native (the yardstick): Kubernetes' published autoscaling rule on the machines (every 15 s; 50% of the request; 10% tolerance; a fall only to the highest count of the last 300 s), the units spread as Kubernetes' scheduler spreads them, all six machines on, as a fixed cluster runs; written by us from its documentation as the measure, it is not Kubernetes itself. Omni on native: Omni-Compass on top of that rule, the engine's strict form as on the kind rigs: the units are the rule's, never fewer; Omni adds a unit the beat work waits in line and past the wall, conveys each unit's CPU wall to the room its machine can spare under 75% (the engine's rule: a unit answers at its machine's speed, not its paper quota's), places by the busy rule, packs, parks and wakes machines. Omni direct: Omni-Compass alone on the machines, no Kubernetes: every 5 s the reflex, the compass law on the 95th percentile, the yardstick twin (what Kubernetes would hold for this work) less the depth the verdict has allowed on the service's own response times (a unit fewer tried under the same traffic, kept only where the response time reads no LOSS within 2%), conveyance, packing, machines parked and woken (two at least; a parked machine takes 10 s to come back).
