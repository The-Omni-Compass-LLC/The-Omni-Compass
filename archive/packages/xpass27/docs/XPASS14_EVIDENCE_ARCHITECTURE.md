## Plant admission theorem (engineering contract)
A candidate plant may be attached to Omni only when all four conditions are represented by executable interfaces:
1. explicit observable state representation;
2. bounded actuation authority;
3. measurable feedback/readback;
4. declared admissibility envelope.
Failure of any condition leaves the plant UNQUALIFIED. A domain name is not evidence.
