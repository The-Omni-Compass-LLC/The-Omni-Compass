# Omni-Compass Complete Organism Discovery and Scale Rule

## Frozen object
The Omni-Compass six-state/eight-line CLAIM1 governing mechanism and controller semantics are frozen. The number of muscles is NOT frozen.

## Discovery variable
Let **N** be the number of materially distinct, admitted control-surface contracts in the completed canonical organism after discovery, deduplication, qualification and evidence labeling.

N is discovered. N is not a target. The historical 640-row tower is a checkpoint, not a ceiling and not a quota.

## Benchmark formula
Breadth is cumulative and importance ordered:

**B(N) = [1, 10, 100, N]**, omitting any prefix value that is not strictly less than N.

After N, no new muscle types are implied. Scale is recursive replication of the complete N-muscle organism:

**S_k(N) = N * 10^k, k = 0,1,2,...**

Therefore the benchmark sequence is:

**1 -> 10 -> 100 -> N -> 10N -> 100N -> 1,000N -> 10,000N -> ...**

At 10N there remain N unique muscle types; there are ten complete N-muscle organisms. At 100N there remain N unique muscle types; there are one hundred complete organisms. Reports MUST separately state `unique_muscle_types=N`, `complete_organism_replicas`, and `active_muscle_instances=N*replicas`.

## Selection rule for 1 / 10 / 100
The 1, 10 and 100 rungs are not random catalog samples. They are cumulative selections ordered by economic consequence, infrastructure criticality, coupling leverage, measurable authority/readback, prevalence, failure consequence, and scientific distinctness. The current intended first lane is Kubernetes supervisory control, followed immediately by GPU/accelerator control and the other highest-value compute/infrastructure organs. Exact muscle contracts remain subject to admission audit.

## Admission rule
A candidate is a distinct muscle only when it exposes a materially distinct controllable authority surface with: observable state; normalization; explicit bounded command; actuation or declared simulator; independent readback; attribution; quantitative success/SLO/resource metrics; restoration; failure behavior; and reproducible receipts. Parameter values of one authority surface are settings, not new muscles.

## Completeness process
Discovery must search across commercially and scientifically consequential controllable systems, including compute/orchestration, accelerators, CPU/memory, networking/fabric, storage/data, AI workload, power/electrical, thermal/cooling, reliability/recovery, distributed/HPC, robotics/industrial, autonomous/flight/space, energy systems, and other domains only where a real control/readback/restore contract exists. Low-value duplicates and decorative knobs are quarantined from the flagship organism even if retained for traceability.

## Evidence discipline
Catalog membership does not imply physical validation. Each muscle carries E0/E1/E2/E3/E4 status. The complete organism can contain specified or simulated muscles, but claims MUST state the evidence class actually earned. Live software and physical claims require corresponding receipts.
