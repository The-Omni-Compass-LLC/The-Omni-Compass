# Full Disclosure / Reproduction Checklist

For every publishable result archive: source commit/hash; frozen engine hash; catalog hash; benchmark rules version; SUT hardware/firmware/software; environment; workload and dataset; seeds; all non-default settings; comparator provenance; pre-registration; start-state receipt; command stream; requested/enforced/current readback; workload/SLO measurements; resource measurements; timing; control effort; violations; failures/refusals; restoration receipt; raw data; analysis code; confidence intervals/effect sizes; adverse/null outcomes; deviations; reproduction command; independent reproducer identity/status.

Energy: modelled energy must never be called measured energy. Device joules must not be called facility energy. Physical energy claims require physical measurement appropriate to the stated boundary.
