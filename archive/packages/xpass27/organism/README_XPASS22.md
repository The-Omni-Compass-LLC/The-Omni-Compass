# XPASS22 Dynamic Complete-Organism Architecture

The Omni-Compass CLAIM1/eight-line mechanism is frozen. Muscle count is not.

The historical 640-row tower is a checkpoint. The complete organism size is **N**, discovered by systematic control-surface discovery, deduplication and admission. The benchmark breadth progression is importance-ordered and cumulative: **1 -> 10 -> 100 -> N**. There is no arbitrary 500 or 1000 breadth rung.

After N, scale means replication of the COMPLETE N-muscle organism, not discovery of more muscle types: **N -> 10N -> 100N -> 1,000N -> 10,000N -> ...**. Reports must distinguish unique muscle types, complete organism replicas, and total active muscle instances.

A trial is one coupled system at its declared hierarchy. Primary paired arms remain NATIVE and OMNI_OVER_NATIVE. No primary OMNI-alone arm is required because Omni is supervisory and native muscles remain the actuators.
