Omni-Compass Live Engine

Left, the organism under its native controllers alone. Right, the same organism on the same demand with Omni-Compass on top. Sixty muscles of a stacked organism, each under its own native controller, each answering on its own clock. One brain on top reads them through nine senses, runs the six-state engine of omni-v7, and writes back through the plug. The distance of each muscle from the ring is its position in its compass band. Scattered is fragmented; on the ring is one coherent state.

How to read it in thirty seconds

  1. Two engines, one demand. Left is the organism with only its native controllers. Right is the same organism, the same demand at the same instant, with Omni-Compass on top. Nothing differs but the brain.
  2. Where a dot sits is how hard that muscle works. On its ring the muscle is at the center of its compass band, p = 0.5. Inward is calmer; outward is closer to its line (p = 1, the dashed red circle). Red means past the line: work is waiting or failing.
  3. The brain is the code. The senses, the six-state engine, the nerves, the compass law and the verdict are ported from the engine's own files and checked against them number for number. The muscles, their demand and their native controllers are a model drawn for this page (class S).

The max: every muscle to its own limit, never below native; the capacity it holds is power given. Tune-down: never more than native, a step back only with the organ’s grant and the release gate; in this model native already sits near its band, so it finds little to hand back: about 0.05% of capacity, every service gauge EVEN; the spread of positions widens by under 0.2% (LOSS on two of three test days), because the calm muscles it hands back move toward their line while the busy ones stay.

Each stress lasts 40 decisions from the moment you press it. Both arms meet the same demand.

Inside the brain

The same state the cycle panel prints, drawn as the physics it is. Each ball is a live value of the Omni-Compass arm, integrated by RK4 every decision. Native has none of this: no state, no memory of need, no stress, no tide; each controller sees only its own reading, one to three decisions late.

U on the double well · eq. (2)

V(U) = −μ(U²/2 − U⁴/4) + (Ė/E_max)·U. A rising E tilts the well toward the failed basin.

S in its potential Φ · eqs. (5)–(6)

Φ(S) = α_s S²/2 + β_s S³/4 − δS. S rolls to S*; the senses pull it back each decision.

The bath B · eq. (7)

B̈ = γ_c δ S − (ω_B/Q_B) Ḃ − ω_B² B. The slow tide of physical load.

The nerves: four scalars multiply into calm

Native has no nerves: nothing grants or refuses a controller, and a blind sense or a security hold changes nothing it does.

calm = κ·h·(1 − (σ − 1)⁺)·(1 − min(ν, 1)). An organ may hand capacity back only when calm ≥ its θ, the line is clean and every sense is live; expanding stays allowed except under a security hold. Under the max, lifting off toward native never needs this grant; going below native does.

The verdict: do no harm, measured

Before a knob moves one step past native, the step runs as a trial: costs at native first, then at the step. omni-v7 reads the change exactly as a report reads a row, on the log of each cost, with its 95% interval. Set what the step truly costs and watch the rule decide.

Native: its knob never moves past its own setting, so there is no trial and nothing to judge; every step Omni-Compass takes past native has to pass this test first. The medians alone allow a step when the trial's median is within the allowance of the reference's. omni-v7 also refuses any step whose interval sits wholly above zero, measures on while the interval is wider than the allowance (up to three times the minimum of 30), and keeps a step only if both estimates are inside the allowance. The parts of the drawing, each a line of the code

  1. 1

    Senses. The muscles' meters are read and normalised to nine observations: q, load, power, thermal, network, drift, stale, security, conflict. adapter.observe_vector. The inner arc of nine segments; steel particles run in along the wires.

  2. 2

    Assimilate. An observed state is formed and the engine is pulled toward it, x ← (1 − a)x + a·x_obs, a = 0.339. adapter.assimilate.

  3. 3

    Evolve. Equations (1) to (7), RK4, ten micro steps of 0.01, the controller evaluated, not applied (u = 0). core.macro_step. The six spokes at the center are E, U, I_U, S, B, Ḃ, each drawn on its own scale: E, U and I_U on [0, 1], S on [−1, 1], B on [−0.5, 1], Ḃ on [−0.5, 0.5].

  4. 4

    Allocate. push = clip(−f_U + 12(1 − U), ±25)/25 and ρ* = clamp(ρ₀ − k_I I_U − k_E E, ρ_min, ρ₀). adapter.Governor.step.

  5. 5

    Nerves. κ, h, σ, ν into calm; calm against each organ's θ grants contraction. nervous_system.scalars, authority, from_governor. On this page each body part answers to one organ: the spine to nodes (θ 0.7), compute to pods (0.5), physics to cpufreq (0.3), energy to power (0.3), distribution to routing (0.5).

  6. 6

    Compass. Each muscle's reading is placed in its band, p = (r − lo)/(hi − lo), and the force is F = A·tanh((k_p(p − ½) + k_d v)/A), failing up past 0.95, never handing back while it climbs above the center; k_d set for critical damping from the muscle's own τ. compass_law.CompassLaw.

  7. 7

    Plug. One wire in, one wire out: each write is clipped to the cover; the snapshot taken once at the start is what reset restores. The engine’s plug also reads each write back and stops if someone else moved the lever; the page’s model takes every write as landed. The kill switch hands every lever to native at once. compass_law.Plug, master.py.

  8. 8

    The verdict. No step past native is kept unless its trial reads no LOSS and its cost sits inside the allowance. verdict.Verdict, judge="interval".

What is the engine's code

The six-state engine, its constants and RK4 integrator, the assimilation, push and ρ*, the four nerve scalars and the organ thresholds, the compass law and the v7 verdict are ported from omnicompass/core.py, adapter.py, nervous_system.py, compass_law.py and verdict.py at commit c8ba7775d, all inside the omni-v7 fingerprint. Checked against the Python on seeded inputs (docs/engine/check_live_engine.py): 8,000 decisions of the governor (state, push, ρ*, κ, h, σ, ν, calm), 48,000 organ grants, 4,800 compass forces, 300 verdict intervals and 400 full verdict trials. Largest difference 2 × 10⁻¹⁵, zero mismatched grants, zero mismatched verdicts.

What is modelled for this page

The body is a drawing model, evidence class S. Sixty muscles named from the 1,107-muscle catalog (twelve each from the spine and the four realms), each a capacity lever with its own response time τ and sensing delay, under an HPA-style native controller (target 0.70, 10% tolerance, five-decision scale-down window). Demand climbs 1 2 3 2 3 4, surges, eases and idles over six hours, the same for both arms. The lever rules that turn F into a write are this page's, written out above and kept inside the doctrine: under the max never below native, lifting off hands back toward native; on the tune-down never above native, and below it only with the organ's grant and the release gate.
