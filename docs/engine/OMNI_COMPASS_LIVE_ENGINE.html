Sixty muscles of a stacked organism from the 1,107-muscle catalog. How to read it in thirty seconds

  1. Two engines, one demand. Left is native alone: the organism with only its native controllers. Right is Omni-Compass on top of native: the same organism and the same native controllers, the same demand at the same instant, with Omni-Compass governing them as brain and nervous system. Nothing differs but Omni-Compass.
  2. Where a dot sits is how hard that muscle works. On its ring the muscle is at the center of its compass band, p = 0.5. Inward is calmer; outward is closer to its line (p = 1, the dashed red circle). Red means past the line: work is waiting or failing.
  3. All of it is the engine's code. The default body is the realms harness itself (realms/harness.py, plants.py, the max in compass_arm_v5.py), recorded by docs/engine/export_harness_trace.py; the brain, nerves, compass law and verdict are ported and checked number for number. A teaching model (class S, drawn for this page, and labelled so) can be switched on to stress the body by hand.

How each row is read: the paired change, native against Omni-Compass on top at the same decision, averaged over blocks of ten decisions so neighbouring decisions do not count twice, with its 95% interval (Student’s t over the blocks). This run: an interval that includes zero reads EVEN; clear of zero, the word follows the gauge’s aim (work: a rise is GAIN; misses, failed work, waiting: a fall is GAIN; energy or capacity held: a rise is power given, a fall a cut). Three runs (tools/readings.py): GAIN or LOSS only when all three runs point the same way with every interval clear of zero; EVEN when an interval includes zero; “the runs disagree” when clear runs point different ways. The spread of positions and the settings are shown, never judged: no rule of the engine aims at them. Run and scored by The Omni-Compass LLC's own code. No outside party has reviewed or approved these results.

4 · Inside the brain

The same state the cycle panel prints, drawn as the physics it is. Each ball is a live value of the Omni-Compass arm, integrated by RK4 every decision. Native has none of this: no state, no memory of need, no stress, no tide; each controller sees only its own reading, one to three decisions late.

U on the double well · eq. (2)

V(U) = −μ(U²/2 − U⁴/4) + (Ė/E_max)·U. A rising E tilts the well toward the failed basin.

S in its potential Φ · eqs. (5)–(6)

Φ(S) = α_s S²/2 + β_s S³/4 − δS. S rolls to S*; the senses pull it back each decision.

The bath B · eq. (7)

B̈ = γ_c δ S − (ω_B/Q_B) Ḃ − ω_B² B. The slow tide of physical load.

The nerves: four scalars multiply into calm

Native has no nerves: nothing grants or refuses a controller, and a blind sense or a security hold changes nothing it does.

5 · The verdict: do no harm, measured

Before a knob moves one step past native, the step runs as a trial: costs at native first, then at the step. omni-v7 reads the change exactly as a report reads a row, on the log of each cost, with its 95% interval. Set what the step truly costs and watch the rule decide.

Native: its knob never moves past its own setting, so there is no trial and nothing to judge; every step Omni-Compass takes past native has to pass this test first. The medians alone allow a step when the trial's median is within the allowance of the reference's. omni-v7 also refuses any step whose interval sits wholly above zero, measures on while the interval is wider than the allowance (up to three times the minimum of 30), and keeps a step only if both estimates are inside the allowance. Ported from omnicompass/verdict.py; the costs are modelled (class S). The code’s default allowance is 0.5%; the slider opens at 2%, the allowance of the table in docs/OMNI_V7.md.

6 · The parts of the drawing, each a line of the code

  1. 1

    Senses. The muscles' meters are read and normalised to nine observations: q, load, power, thermal, network, drift, stale, security, conflict. adapter.observe_vector. The inner arc of nine segments; steel particles run in along the wires.

  2. 2

    Assimilate. An observed state is formed and the engine is pulled toward it, x ← (1 − a)x + a·x_obs, a = 0.339. adapter.assimilate.

  3. 3

    Evolve. Equations (1) to (7), RK4, ten micro steps of 0.01, the controller evaluated, not applied (u = 0). core.macro_step. The six spokes at the center are E, U, I_U, S, B, Ḃ, each drawn on its own scale: E, U and I_U on [0, 1], S on [−1, 1], B on [−0.5, 1], Ḃ on [−0.5, 0.5].

  4. 4

    Allocate. push = clip(−f_U + 12(1 − U), ±25)/25 and ρ* = clamp(ρ₀ − k_I I_U − k_E E, ρ_min, ρ₀). adapter.Governor.step.

  5. 5

    Nerves. κ, h, σ, ν into calm; calm against each organ's θ grants contraction. nervous_system.scalars, authority, from_governor. On this page each body part answers to one organ: the spine to nodes (θ 0.7), compute to pods (0.5), physics to cpufreq (0.3), energy to power (0.3), distribution to routing (0.5).

  6. 6

    Compass. Each muscle's reading is placed in its band, p = (r − lo)/(hi − lo), and the force is F = A·tanh((k_p(p − ½) + k_d v)/A), failing up past 0.95, never handing back while it climbs above the center; k_d set for critical damping from the muscle's own τ. compass_law.CompassLaw.

  7. 7

    Plug. One wire in, one wire out: each write is clipped to the cover; the snapshot taken once at the start is what reset restores. The engine’s plug also reads each write back and stops if someone else moved the lever; the page’s model takes every write as landed. The kill switch hands every lever to native at once. compass_law.Plug, master.py.

  8. 8

    The verdict. In the live controller, the trigger that keeps a step past native only when it costs nothing; in the realms harness the same rule is kept by the twin and the bone (nothing below native, nothing past the line’s room). No step past native is kept unless its trial reads no LOSS and its cost sits inside the allowance. verdict.Verdict, judge="interval".

What is the engine's code

The six-state engine, its constants and RK4 integrator, the assimilation, push and ρ*, the four nerve scalars and the organ thresholds, the compass law and the v7 verdict are ported from omnicompass/core.py, adapter.py, nervous_system.py, compass_law.py and verdict.py at commit c8ba7775d, all inside the omni-v7 fingerprint. Checked against the Python on seeded inputs (docs/engine/check_live_engine.py): 8,000 decisions of the governor (state, push, ρ*, κ, h, σ, ν, calm), 48,000 organ grants, 4,800 compass forces, 300 verdict intervals and 400 full verdict trials. Largest difference 2 × 10⁻¹⁵, zero mismatched grants, zero mismatched verdicts.

The two bodies

The realms harness (the default). The sixty muscles are one organism of realms/harness.py: each a plant of realms/plants.py (machine pools under their autoscalers, cooled zones, batteries on their lines, motion axes, process loops), coupled through their heat and their power, under the climb (1 2 3 2 3 4 …), 240 decisions of 15 s. Omni-Compass on top is the max of realms/compass_arm_v5.py with the body’s bone, its twins and the muscles’ cards; at 80% the brain goes silent and the lease walks every setting back to native over 24 decisions. Recorded on seeds 3000, 3001 and 3002 (runs A, B and C) by docs/engine/export_harness_trace.py; the page replays the record. In that harness the brain’s state is computed from the body’s aggregate, as a governor reads it, and shown; the max itself acts muscle by muscle. The teaching model. A drawing model, evidence class S. Sixty muscles named from the 1,107-muscle catalog (twelve each from the spine and the four realms), each a capacity lever with its own response time τ and sensing delay, under an HPA-style native controller (target 0.70, 10% tolerance, five-decision scale-down window). Demand climbs 1 2 3 2 3 4, surges, eases and idles over six hours, the same for both arms. The lever rules that turn F into a write are this page's, written out above and kept inside the doctrine: under the max never below native, lifting off hands back toward native; on the tune-down never above native, and below it only with the organ's grant and the release gate.
